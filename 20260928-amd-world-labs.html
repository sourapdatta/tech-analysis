AMD bought a company Nvidia already backs, at 1.5–1.6× its last price

How much AMD paid for World Labs against the company's last valuation and other 2026 world-model prices, and which chipmakers already back the category. Prompted by AMD's all-stock agreement in September 2026.

28-Sep-2026 · data: AMD and NVIDIA filings and newsrooms, Bloomberg, Forbes, TechCrunch, SiliconANGLE, company announcements

Bottom line. AMD's $8.2B deal is the highest closed 2026 world-model valuation, yet only 1.5–1.6× World Labs' last reported valuation, against 2.9× for Nvidia's Hugging Face and Groq deals. Nvidia is in 4 of 6 closed 2026 world-model rounds and AMD in 3 of 6, with both in World Labs and Runway. The implication: a chipmaker stake in a world-model lab now looks like an option to buy it.

What the data shows

1

AMD's $8.2B tops the ranked 2026 world-model valuations

World Labs' own February round is reported at $5.0–5.4B, Runway at $5.3B, AMI Labs at about $4.5B after its $1.03B raise, Decart at $4.0B, General Intuition at $2.3B and Odyssey at $1.45B. Emulate (unclosed) and General Intuition's reported new round are left out.

2

At 1.5–1.6×, the step-up is about half Nvidia's 2.9×

Nvidia's Hugging Face and Groq deals were priced at 2.9× each over their last valuations. Anthropic's abandoned bid of roughly $6B for Decart implied 1.5× on its $4.0B round, close to what AMD paid for World Labs.

3

Nvidia is in 4 of 6 rounds, AMD in 3, both in the top two

Nvidia is named in rounds covering about 81% of the $3.3B raised and AMD in about 50%. Both are in World Labs and Runway, the two most valuable companies. Only General Intuition, led by Khosla, names neither.

What it means for leadership

  • Read the premium against the clock. World Labs' last mark was only months old, so 1.5–1.6× annualises to 2.0–2.3× a year, against 1.4× a year for Hugging Face over three years.
  • Stock made the deal cheaper for AMD. In late February World Labs' valuation equalled about 1.5–1.7% of AMD's market cap; the $8.2B is about 0.8% of it now, because AMD's market cap is up 3.2×. The share count floats until closing.
  • Nvidia likely gets paid in AMD stock. Its venture arm backed World Labs earlier and it joined the February round; terms for existing investors are undisclosed, and each 1% held converts to about $82M of AMD stock at the deal price.
  • Stakes look like options, but insiders still pay up. AMD held World Labs stock and Nvidia was in Hugging Face's 2023 round, yet Nvidia paid 2.9× for Hugging Face. Three cases show a pattern, not a rule.

Read-across for other buyers

  • Next candidates. Runway (both chipmakers), AMI Labs and Decart (Nvidia) and Odyssey (AMD) are the companies where a chipmaker already holds a stake. One outlet reports Nvidia approached Decart before Anthropic did.
  • Amazon is a third chip player. It invested in Odyssey, which named AWS its preferred cloud, and is a strategic customer of Decart.

Caveats and method

  • World Labs' last valuation is reported by Bloomberg and Forbes, not confirmed by the company, and sources differ ($5.0B versus $5.4B post-money); the step-up is therefore a range. If the $5.0B figure excluded new money the step-up would be about 1.4×.
  • Groq's prior valuation is single-source and graded low; the Decart bid was never completed; Nvidia's stated Hugging Face price is not described as cash in its filing.
  • Six rounds is a small sample. Participation reflects investors named in announcements, which are partial, and dollar shares use whole-round size, not the chipmakers' own cheques.
  • The AMD-currency comparison uses AMD's market cap in late February as a proxy for the round date, and AMD's final share count is set by its average price before closing.
  • The ranking uses closed rounds on a post-money basis. General Intuition is reported to be in talks at $6B pre-money, and Emulate's round at $3.7B is unclosed.
